About Michael Polley

Mike Polley is a semiconductor and AI technology executive with 30 years of R&D and business leadership at Samsung and Texas Instruments. His work has centered on one question: how to turn breakthrough technology into product differentiation and market advantage.

He is an IEEE Fellow, a TI Fellow and an MIT Ph.D., with more than 50 U.S. patents. Boards, C-suites and investors engage him to evaluate emerging technologies, shape differentiation strategy, judge development timing and assess likely market impact.

He founded Polley Advisory Group in 2026 to bring that experience to investors and companies making high-stakes technology decisions.
